# MTS Game Jam 2026 — "Borrowed Time"

## Brief

**Theme:** Borrowed Time

**Judging criteria** (each one is a box we have to tick on purpose):

| Criterion | What judges ask | How we design for it |
|---|---|---|
| Repeatability | Would players play again? | Short runs, a score or time to beat, variety from run to run |
| Relevance to Theme | Does it fit the theme? | The theme is the **core mechanic**, not just the title or story |
| Fun Factor | Is it enjoyable? | Core loop is fun in the first 30 seconds, with game feel and juice |
| Visuals | Appealing and consistent? | One narrow art style and palette, used everywhere |
| Bugs | Does everything work? | Small scope, a feature freeze, a full day of polish and testing |
| Originality | Something creative? | Throw away the first obvious idea (countdown timers, rewind) |
| Intuitiveness | Controls easy to understand? | ≤ 4 inputs, taught by play, not by a wall of text |
| Storytelling | Tells a story effectively? | Environmental or mechanical story, told in short beats |
| Sound Design | Sound and music help? | SFX on every action, one looping track, an audio cue for the theme mechanic |
| Balance of Difficulty | Is the challenge fair? | A difficulty curve, playtested by people who aren't us |

**Allowed platforms:** Scratch, Unity, Unreal, Godot, GameMaker, CryEngine, Roblox Studio, Pygame, Arcade, Panda3D, UPBGE, Ursina

## Working principles
- **Execution over the perfect idea.** A polished small game beats an ambitious broken one.
- **Cut scope early.** Decide the MVP on day 1 and keep a "nice to have" list we only touch once the MVP is done.
- **Stick to the concept.** No restarts after the idea is locked.
- **Quality over quantity.** 1 mechanic × 10 levels of depth beats 10 mechanics × 1 level.
